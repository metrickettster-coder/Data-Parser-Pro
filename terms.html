The service

DataParser Pro is a browser-based utility that cleans and structures pasted or uploaded text (CSV, logs, source code, key:value data) into JSON, CSV, or Markdown. All parsing happens locally in your browser — see the Privacy Policy for details. Pricing and billing

The first 5 rows of any result are free to preview. Beyond that, two paid options are available, both processed by Stripe:

  • One-time unlock ($9): a single payment that unlocks unlimited full exports for the rest of that browser session.
  • Power subscription ($4.99/month): a recurring monthly charge that additionally unlocks batch file processing, combined .zip export, and expanded saved history.

We don't store your card details — Stripe handles all payment information directly.

Canceling the Power subscription

You can cancel anytime, and cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. Two ways to do it:

  • Use the Manage Subscription link in the app (visible once Power is active) or in your Stripe receipt email.
  • If you can't find that link, reply to any Stripe receipt email from a purchase, or reach us through the feedback form on the homepage, and we'll cancel it for you.

Refunds
